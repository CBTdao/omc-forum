Contact

One small editorial team reads everything sent through the channels below. Corrections get the fastest response, because they change what we publish.

Updated October 4, 2026

Ways to reach the editors

  • Email — editor@omc.center. One address for everything: corrections, tips, source documents, permissions, privacy requests.
  • Comments — the giscus thread on any article. Public by design; the editors read every thread.
  • X / Twitter — @OmniVerseCompute. Good for quick pointers; not good for corrections, which deserve a written record.

What we welcome

  • Corrections — a wrong number, an outdated claim, a source that doesn't say what we said it says. Verified corrections are fixed in place and noted in the article.
  • Primary sources — pricing pages, order book screenshots with timestamps, filings, benchmarks. If it documents a claim, we want it.
  • Disagreement — the forum runs on arguments. If you think a piece is wrong in its reasoning rather than its data, say so; the good ones become follow-up articles.
  • Permission requests — quoting with attribution needs no permission; for republication, ask and the answer is usually yes.

What we cannot do

We are editors, not advisers. We cannot tell you whether to buy a specific token, which GPU to procure for your workload, or how to treat any of it in your taxes. Nothing here is financial, investment, legal or tax advice — see the terms of use. For individual decisions, talk to a licensed professional who knows your situation.

Privacy requests

Access and deletion requests under GDPR/CCPA also come through the channels above. For context on what data the forum holds (very little) and what Google and GitHub hold (their policies govern), read the privacy policy first.
